What to Do in Sri Lanka

Discover unforgettable experiences across the island.

Climb Sigiriya Rock

Explore the ancient rock fortress and admire panoramic views of lush landscapes.

Visit Tea Plantations

Walk through scenic tea estates in Nuwara Eliya and taste world-famous Ceylon tea.

Relax on the Beaches

Unwind at golden sandy beaches like Mirissa, Unawatuna, and Arugam Bay.

Go Whale Watching

Spot majestic blue whales and playful dolphins off the coast of Mirissa.

Safari in National Parks

See wild elephants, leopards, and exotic birds in Yala or Udawalawe National Park.

Visit Ancient Temples

Experience peace and history at the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy or Anuradhapura’s sacred sites.

Scenic Train Ride

Take the iconic blue train from Kandy to Ella through tea-covered hills and waterfalls.
